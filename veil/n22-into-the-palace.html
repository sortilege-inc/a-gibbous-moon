Night 22 — Into the Palace

☾
Runnable kit — the second of the campaign's last four sessions. In-world: days after the Grand Negotiation broke up; the coup is rolling underneath everything. Job: force the party to commit an aim, then open a road to the Harem and Bergüzar — the campaign's moral hinge. Runtime ~3h: 20 min commit-the-aim · 70 min working the ways in · 45 min the sister-meeting (or seeding it) · 30 min the clocks turning · 15 min land-and-tease. Follows Night 21; live state in Open Threads.

The Negotiation answered the tactical question (who allies, who opposes) and exposed the strategic one the party has kept dodging: what are they actually for? They lean to the Ghost but swore to nothing, and there are only three sessions left. Night 22 turns the campaign from "set the table" to "reach the throne" — get the party to name an aim, then spend the night opening a way into the palace before the coup goes loud. The buried spine: with Mira in hand, the party can propose sparing Bergüzar rather than removing her — the choice the whole finale bends around.

The through-line to hold

Track one line all night: commit → approach → the sister. First force the aim; then open one solid road into the Harem (the harem channel, the cats, or the mint); then get Mira and Bergüzar toward the same room — the meeting that decides whether "spare her" is even possible. Everything happens against a coup already in motion: a clock, not a question.

Part 1 — Force the aim (~20 min)

Open by making them say it out loud. They walked out of the Negotiation without committing; don't let Night 22 drift into more errands. Put the question plainly and get a table answer.

The aim they pickWhat it commits them to
Spare Bergüzar / avert the Wish (the built-toward spine)Steer the Ghost's coup toward the assembly, reach Bergüzar, and offer Mira as the sister who can talk her down or take her place. The moral finale. Everything below serves this.
Back the Ghost's coup outrightThey stop hedging and help the Sekban win — faster, bloodier, and it makes them complicit in whatever Alemdar does to the Harem. A darker, simpler road.
Break with the GhostThey decide the coup is the greater evil and work to blunt or expose it — which throws them toward the Padishah or toward Ahra, both of whom they like less.
Protect Mira only, let the city burnDisengage from the politics, keep their charge alive, ride out the coup. Valid — but the endgame then happens to them.

Whatever they choose, name it back to them and hold them to it. The rest of the kit assumes the spine (spare Bergüzar); swap the destination if they pick otherwise, but the machinery of getting into the palace is the same.

Part 2 — Reaching the Harem (the ways in, ~70 min)

Three roads. They need only one to work, but each has a price and a risk. Let them split up to push more than one.

RoadWho works itWhat it buysThe catch
The harem channelGizemA message carried to Bergüzar from inside — through the wet-nurse Emine, the coffee-mistress Dilrûba, or the concubine Gulnaz. The harem is already on edge and the Haseki visibly troubled.Emine is watched — Aziz's man tailed her home. Push too hard and the channel burns, or leads a hunter straight to Mira.

The same Crown also buys Sharif's secret (who told him of Mira).They lose the Crown for good, and hand a serpent-adjacent faction a foothold in the palace. Decide before they knock.

A wholly new, respectable way toward the palace.Untested, and his own agenda is unknown. He stayed behind at the Negotiation; he wants something. Find out what before leaning on him.

Assets in hand: the Crown (for the cats), Gizem's harem "in" (but Emine is watched), Bedros's letter and grudge-free footing, Osman's papers, and — if they finish it — the Lodge book as an unreachable pocket-study for Mira while they operate.

Part 3 — The moral hinge: Mira ↔ Bergüzar (~45 min)

  • This is the heart of the endgame. The untrained sister who could talk the Haseki Sultan down, take her place, or refuse both. Engineering a secret meeting — under the cover of the coup's chaos, or through the harem channel — is the play that makes "spare Bergüzar" real.
  • Mira is non-committal about reunion. Don't play her as eager. She resents being the sister left behind; she does not believe her sister wants her dead, but knows others would kill her to profit the Haseki. Whether she'll even sit down is a scene in itself.
  • The interference cuts both ways. The nearer Mira draws to the palace, the more Bergüzar's power thins and her distress worsens — which is driving the raids. A meeting inside the palace is dangerous precisely because it works. Use her visions as the emotional bridge: Bergüzar has been begging the party, in dreams, to avert the world's end.
  • If the meeting can't land this session, seed it — a reply carried back, a token, a place and hour set for Night 23.

Part 4 — The clocks turning underneath

  • The coup is in motion. Alemdar's Sekban move with or without the party, fed by Papa Mert's Greek fire. Anything loud at the palace collides with the timing — a coup that goes off while Mira sits a wall from Bergüzar's failing power is a disaster you can time deliberately.
  • The Ahra fuse. Ahra now openly opposes the Ghost and still does not know he killed her father. Decide in advance whether you're holding the lid or lighting it — and what tips it (a Lodge record of the greatest good, an old comrade, a loose word). Lighting it is the single biggest board-changer you hold.
  • The blind spot. Hano and Murad were left uninvited and unwatched. Let them act off-screen — a raid, a claim staked, "Ahmet" clocked as non-human — so the party's choice to skip them costs something.
  • Aziz surfaces. He didn't disrupt the Negotiation; he's overdue. The palace approach is exactly where A Certain Community's watch and the Padishah's search converge. Crystallize Aziz as the finale's near-enemy here.

Part 5 — Landing it → what N23 needs

Aim to leave the session with three things fixed:

  1. A committed aim (Part 1), named and held.
  2. One road into the Harem open — the cats bought, the harem channel working, or Bedros engaged — and the Crown question resolved either way.
  3. The sister-meeting landed or seeded, and Aziz squarely in the way as the obstacle Night 23 must beat to reach Bergüzar before the Lunar Eclipse.

If it stalls, the coup's clock and the Sultan's worsening distress force the issue: the raids widen, the palace tightens, and events start happening to the party instead of by them.

Quick-reference (one glance): Open by forcing the aim · roads in = harem channel (Emine, watched) · cats (spend the Crown) · mint (Bedros, agenda unknown) · hinge = Mira ↔ Bergüzar (she's reluctant; her power thins Bergüzar's) · clocks = coup rolling · Ahra fuse (hold or light) · Hano/Murad off-screen · Aziz = finale enemy.
