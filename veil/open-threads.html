Open Threads & the Endgame

☾
Live campaign state — current through Night 19. The one page here that is not module prep but the actual running dossier: what is open, who is moving against whom, and where it all points. Update it as play advances.

The party is Level 7, allied with the Ghost of March, and has reached Mira — the one woman who can cripple the Haseki Sultan — winning her fragile trust in the wreck of her own animate house, only for a sealed royal warrant to arrest and execute her to change every stake at once. Now they must smuggle the Sultan's sister off the farm and into the Undercity before the next wave arrives, while behind them the city still holds a crowd of loose ends: a half-freed serpent, an escaped mage, a mole in the palace, a vendetta waiting to catch fire. This is the ledger of everything still in play.

State of play

The Cabal (Level 7): Gizem Faruki (dervish monk), Miray (druid), Jean-Zera Mongé (bard), Sidra al-Najjar — now a djinn-master warlock since her djinn Faravi was torn loose. Alliance: the Ghost of March / Alemdar. Now travelling with Mira — the Sultan's fugitive sister, newly won over — bound for the Undercity. Jean-Zera and Gizem are deafened for a few hours by a rebound of Mira's grief (clears before next session).

The main quest — Mira found

  • Reached and won over (Night 19). The party found Mira at her farm near the Trojan dig site, defended her against a second Bostanji force, and — through Jean-Zera, Osman's journal, and the shared dreams — earned her fragile trust. She is a powerful, frightened diviner: illusion and decoy-doubles, teleportation of others, blur, an evil-eye curse that struck Sidra mute, and frequent spell-rebounds (one of which deafened Jean-Zera and Gizem). Handle her as fragile and dangerous both.
  • The royal death-warrant. The captured captain bore a sealed, formal writ — not an arrest order but a warrant to take Mira and execute her, under the authority of the Padishah himself. Whether this is Mejid's true will or his seal turned to another's purpose is the campaign's next hinge — and it makes the "bring her to the city" path far more perilous.
  • The Living Manor. Her house is a gargantuan animate construct that fought intruder and rescuer alike, and is bound to its ground: it will not leave, but is compelled to give chase if Mira is stolen from more than 500 ft. Left behind at the farm. (Stats & the encounter: Mira & the Living Manor.)
  • What she confirmed. Osman visited her, was kind, took his notes in that journal, urged her to stay on the farm and seek no one, warned she is a danger to all near her, and swore to protect her only so long as she stayed — the matter-and-antimatter interference that dampens Bergüzar's power the nearer Mira draws. She does not believe her sister wants her dead, but knows others would kill her to profit the Haseki Sultan; whether Bergüzar is complicit is a separate, open question.
  • The sisters' rift. As children, their father raised a hand to Karia (Bergüzar's birth name); their mother killed him with a clock — Karia having magically bent her arm to strike the harder. Blame fell on Karia; Mira was left behind, resentful, when Karia was taken away for training. Mira is not seeking reunion and is markedly non-committal about it. She is the untrained sister; Karia had the schooling.
  • The plan the party landed on: extract Mira, hide her in an Undercity hamam (a place of discretion — Miray's bathhouse contacts), work toward a secret meeting of the sisters out of the Padishah's sight, and — long term — proper training, mooted as a university. Alexander's ward Shehrazat is a parallel case and a possible ally, though the Ghost's shadow over that quarter, and Bergüzar's ability to sense Mira's nearness through the dampening, are live risks.

The Lodge of Byzantia (unused)

Osman signed his letter Loremaster of the Lodge of Byzantia. The party has the Lodge's address, its new-moon meeting time, and — through Faravi, who was summoned by a Lodge ritual — two of its secret gestures. They hold a credential and a way in they have not yet spent.

Loose dangers

Things left alive, loose, or unresolved that can resurface:

  • The half-freed Shahmeran. Two of four chains broken; mobile in her cell and able to cast, her "obligation" declared fulfilled. Contact runs through Faravi — an ancient serpentess now has a line into Sidra's own bond.
  • The escaped mage. The recruited prisoner who cast Cone of Cold, turned invisible, and fled — a powerful caster who knows the Sublime Porte's methods, and no one is tracking him.
  • The palace mole. An unnamed official fled the peace talks after Kavalan's murder of Hamit; both the party and Mejid noticed.
  • Kavalan's leverage. He killed Hamit publicly and cast Speak with Dead — putting Jean-Zera's description on record and implicating the palace. Talks continue, strained.
  • The snake-eyed vizier left alive in the cistern (believes he serves Mehmed the Conqueror); Baghatur, the Khazarian trapped in a scroll; and the Medusa Arcadia, the petrified guardian in the exit path — a tragic figure per Petrus Gyllius's diary. Any of the three can return.
  • Eve the Unchained. Seeded, not yet met: a half-elf fostered by the elf druid Greenhat, gathering followers to eat "the fruit in chains" from the Garden of Eden, tied to the sick cats and red eggs. The Ghost views her as a ticking threat. Byzas, the tiefling who warned of her by a Sending, was found poisoned at the Lake of Sukut — his poisoning knotted to the red-egg thread.
  • Alexander's vendetta. He knows Ahra killed his wife Nadia for using magic. Set aside for now, but a powder keg under the Janissary–Sekban mediation the Ghost wants.
  • Kore's true nature — a Darkness-weaving shade — remains unknown to most of the party.

Faction pressure

  • The Ghost / Alemdar wants the party to broker Sekban–Janissary unity through Ahra while keeping up the appearance of enmity. Not yet acted on — and Ahra's kill-the-casters creed cuts against the pluralist alliance he wants. How this mediation goes shapes whether Act Four's last fight is against Ahra alone or against Alemdar himself.
  • A Certain Community / Aziz Sefa is the live antagonist: entangled with the party from Yedikule, implicated in the library-looting, and now openly hunting Mira. Aziz fog-ringed away from the Night 18 ambush and was not present at the manor; the Night 19 attackers were a fresh Bostanji column with Community agents attached, bearing the royal writ. This was already the second wave out of Constantinople, and — the party has realised — more are coming. Aziz will surface again, and next time he knows the party has Mira.

Side errands still open

  • Crown of Sargon — claimed (Nights 17–18); it remade Sidra and commanded the dragon Yutba off. Its ties to the Ninth Life and the sick-cats web remain.
  • Alexander & Shehrazat — Alexander agreed to tutor Shehrazat if hidden from both Ahra and Alemdar; his short-term lodging at Papa Mert's has likely lapsed and needs a longer arrangement. Shehrazat was also told to seek Dr. Feuerbach at the Pera Palace Hotel for her unstable magic, but cannot go above ground herself.
  • The Spectral Hippodrome — Susanna and Chique Manu's race-fixing; a promised new race pending; the Blue Rider's departure has destabilized the Blue/Green balance.
  • The sick cats & red eggs — Sharif's unanswered request, knotted to Eve, the Ninth Life, and the Crown.
  • Giannis the smuggler — expects further treasure-brokering; Jean-Zera's tie to him is publicly burned.
  • Miray's druid network — Deniz, a druid known only in goat form who stewards an Undercity oasis, remains unvisited — a possible source of nature-lore on the red eggs and Eve's followers.
  • The Ermeydani — the Undercity's no-weapons fighting arena with heavy betting; mapped in Sidra's survey but never visited, a venue for coin, a test of skill, or overheard rumour.

Where it all points

The reach-Mira arc is now done; the near arc has become get her out and hide her, and decide what she is for — a shield for the Haseki Sultan, a lever against her, or a fugitive to be kept quietly alive. That feeds directly into The Grand Negotiation, where the succession is decided and the party sets the endgame table — now complicated by a royal warrant that may or may not be the Sultan's true will. From there the module drives toward The Eyes of Suleiman: the Ghost's plan is to sever Mejid from the Harem by removing Bergüzar, but her visions — and now Mira herself, the sister who could talk her down or take her place — give the party the chance to propose sparing her instead, the campaign's moral hinge. Then the Lunar Eclipse and one of the three finales (see The Acts). On the current alliance, that means clearing the Janissary Remnants and, at the very last, a reckoning with Alemdar — unless the mediation with Ahra, or a change of heart, rewrites the ending.

☾ Next Session (proposed)

☾
Proposal for Night 20 — "the most powerful fugitive in the land." A getaway-and-go-to-ground session: the tense overland-and-underground run to hide Mira, threaded with her grief, the sisters' history, and the question of the warrant. Options, not rails — the party has already chosen the Undercity hamam as the destination, so let them earn it.

Opening beat — leaving the farm

  • The clock is running. Open on the immediate aftermath: bodies to deal with, two guards fled (one may report, one the djinn ran down), Jean-Zera and Gizem still deaf for an hour or so. Mira agrees to come but will not be hurried, and cannot take much. The Living Manor cannot follow, and grieves to be left — a genuinely mournful parting, and a lever: it will hunt anyone who steals her, so it must be clear she goes willingly.
  • What Mira brings. Decide what leaves the house with her — the phantom cat (a years-dead pet she cannot let go of), a keepsake or two, perhaps a divination that gives the party one useful glimpse. Her power travels; her house does not.

The run to the Undercity

  • Pursuit. This is the second wave; seed the third. A tracker or scout on their trail, a checkpoint on the road, or a Community watcher at the crossing back to the city — one hard choice about whether to fight, hide (Miray's silence), or talk their way through. Mira's magic is a wild card: powerful, panicky, and rebound-prone under stress.
  • The dampening tell. The nearer Mira comes to the city, the more Bergüzar's power thins — which Bergüzar (and anyone watching her) can feel. Reaching Constantinople may itself ring an alarm in the Harem. Play the Haseki Sultan reacting from afar.
  • Going to ground. Miray's Undercity bathhouse contact — a place of discretion where a face seen is forgotten. Establish the safehouse, its keeper, its price, and its blind spots. Note the proximity risks: the Ghost of March holds that quarter, and Alexander shelters Shehrazat nearby — a natural ally (two untrained girls, two hidden gifts) but also a second secret to keep.

Threads to pull

  • The warrant. Give the party a way to test it: is the death-order truly Mejid's, or a faction abusing his seal? Gizem's earlier written appeal to the head of the Harem is still out and could return an answer. This is the session's central mystery.
  • The sister-meeting. Begin laying the ground for a secret Mira–Bergüzar meeting the party floated — who carries the message, where it could safely happen, and whether Mira can be talked past her reluctance.
  • The dreams & the Wish. With Mira now in reach, the party may press her on the shared visions and the apocalyptic crowned-son Wish; she is the diviner best placed to read them.
  • Colour & consequence. The deaf hours as a complication mid-run; Mira testing whether these strangers will still protect her when it is inconvenient; the moral weight of hiding "a suitcase-nuke" — a person who is also a weapon.

Where it should land: Mira hidden (for now), the warrant's provenance at least partly answered, and the sister-meeting or the Grand Negotiation set as the next horizon. If the party stalls, the third wave arriving at the empty farm — and then in the city — keeps the pressure on.
