Night 21 — The Grand Negotiation

☾
Runnable kit — the first of the campaign's last four sessions. In-world: four days after the Ghost's summons, ~a week after Mira was brought home. Venue: the neutral, well-kept "derelict" tavern the Ghost named — cloth-draped tables, one long board, no faction's ground. Job: set the endgame board — who allies, who opposes, and commit the party to an aim. Runtime ~3h: 45 min run-up · 90 min the table · 30 min the disruption · 15 min land-and-tease. Part of the four-session arc.

The Ghost is using this table to unite the city's cast-out behind his coup. The buried bomb: Ahra does not know the Ghost is the man whose explosion orphaned her. Getting her to sit at his table without it detonating is the emotional spine of the night. Everything else — Mira, the raids, the succession — hangs off whether the pluralists cohere or the anti-magic bloc splits them.

The through-line to hold

Track one line all night: pluralist ↔ anti-magic. The pluralists (the Ghost, Sharif's cats, a won-over Deniz) want a city where the magical and the non-human are left in peace; the anti-magic bloc (Hano and her strongman Murad, and whatever of Ahra's kill-the-casters creed survives) wants them leashed. The party is the swing, and they hold the trump: Mira.

The agenda (what is actually on the table)

  1. A united front — the Ghost's ask; Ahra's inclusion is the crux, Hano/Murad the wedge.
  2. The succession & the heir — the party alone knows the Wish stake; how much do they reveal?
  3. Mira — everyone suspects the party holds the key; her fate is the trump card. How much do they show?
  4. The Undercity vs the raids — the pluralist haven against the anti-magic crackdown.

Part 3 — Running the room

  • Treat it as a positional contest, not one roll. Track each faction's lean on the pluralist ↔ anti-magic line. Persuasion/Insight/Intimidation (DC 13–16 by disposition) nudges a faction one step; a blunder or a Hano gambit nudges it back. Deniz's accord and Sharif (if bought) start pluralist; Hano/Murad start hostile; Ahra and Chique Manu are the swingable middle.
  • The party's trump is Mira — revealing they hold her wins leverage but paints a target; keeping her hidden keeps them safe but weakens their hand. Make them weigh it.
  • The Ahra/Ghost fuse: every scene near the two of them, call for the party to actively keep the lid on (Insight to spot Ahra's dawning suspicion; a quick word to defuse). If they don't tend it, it slips toward the disruption.

Part 4 — The disruption (pick one; ~mid-table)

Keep it from being a flat talk-scene.

  • (Recommended) A Certain Community moves. Aziz's people found the venue (Giannis sold it, or they tailed Emine). A spy in the room, or a raid at the doors mid-negotiation — forcing the factions to scatter or bond under fire. Cleanest way to forge the coalition and crystallize Aziz as the finale enemy. Ties off the "Emine is watched" thread with teeth.
  • Alexander crashes it hunting Ahra (the Nadia vendetta) — the party must manage the blood-feud the Ghost needs buried, in front of everyone.
  • Hano/Murad's gambit — Hano outs Mira, or "Ahmet's" nature, or Murad arrives with warriors to strong-arm the table anti-magic.

You can stack the recommended raid with the Ahra/Ghost fuse: under attack, Ahra fights beside the Ghost — and starts to wonder who he really is.

Part 5 — Landing it → what N22 needs

Aim to leave the table with three things fixed:

  1. A coalition (who is in) and the party's committed aim — protect Mira, and (seed it here) the first whisper of sparing Bergüzar.
  2. The cats resolved (Crown traded or not → whether palace intel is in hand for the infiltration).
  3. The pivot set: the plan to reach Bergüzar / the palace — the N22 infiltration. Hano/Murad + Aziz crystallized as the opposition to beat.

If it fractures instead of coheres, that is fine — it just means N22 opens with the party working a thinner coalition and a bolder infiltration.

Quick-reference (one glance): Ghost = unite + remove Bergüzar · Ahra = restore corps, DON'T reveal the Ghost · Hano/Murad = anti-magic wedge · Sharif = Undercity autonomy (Crown?) · Manu = backs the winner · Trump = Mira · Fuse = Ahra ↔ Ghost · Bomb = Aziz raid.
